FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. MirrorLink is free and open source (MIT). There are no paid tiers, no subscription, and no ads.

Does it require internet?

No. Everything runs between your phone and PC over your local Wi-Fi. Internet access is only needed to download the apps in the first place.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Connections are encrypted with WebRTC DTLS-SRTP, pairing uses one-time codes, and your screen data never leaves your own network.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 10 and newer (API 24+). The Windows app requires Windows 10 or 11.
